# Apex UGR Product Spec

Apex UGR is an invitation-only automotive network with game-style progression layered over real vehicle ownership, lawful driving, meets, convoys, and verified closed-course competition.

## Primary Destinations

- HUD: identity, active build, Daily Chest, network pulse, COTW, and operations.
- Garage: real vehicles, media, digital capture, Mod Sync, and vehicle-specific parts.
- Map: GPS, routes, Driver Mode, exploration, caches, events, drivers, crews, and Bounties.
- Race: solo telemetry and route/relay competition on lawful courses.
- Leaders: privacy-aware verified boards across performance and community systems.
- Meets: discovery, creation, registration, check-in, showcase, and convoys.
- Shop: Ghost Credits, cosmetic inventory, equip state, trading, and referrals.
- Social: virtualized vertical media feed, follows, comments, likes, saves, profiles, and comms.

## Product Rules

- Purchases cannot buy REP, rank, wins, or earned achievements.
- A sold cosmetic must produce an observable equipped visual state.
- Bounty participation is opt-in, Driver Mode gated, privacy-preserving, and intended only for authorized legal venues.
- No live feature may fabricate human users, records, speed, GPS, race results, rewards, or commerce success.
- Bounty events may use clearly labeled NPC runners and hunters only to fill roles left open by live opted-in drivers. NPCs must never impersonate a human player.
- Empty states are valid outside the scheduled Bounty fallback system; fake human activity is not.

## URL Contract

Canonical section URLs use descriptive hierarchy, including `/app/competition/races`, `/app/competition/leaderboards`, `/app/events/meets`, `/app/map/bounty`, and `/app/social/crews`. Common historical aliases remain supported and normalize to their canonical destination after navigation.
